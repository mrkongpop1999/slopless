# Art direction — Opening: ประวัติศาสตร์นาฏศิลป์ไทย สมัยรัตนโกสินทร์ (Paper-cut Lightbox × slopless-2d)

## Audience & platform
Class/teacher presentation, 16:9 1920x1080, Thai. Opening establishing shot, ~6 s, no hook needed.

## Story beat
Dusk over a palace hall. Oil lamps along the stage light one by one, the paper screen behind the stage glows,
a lakhon dancer's silhouette begins a slow ram. The title is cut in cream paper on the dark front band.
Nod to หนังใหญ่ (shadow theatre): the dancer is read as a silhouette against a glowing screen.

## Mood (3 words)
graceful, warm, ceremonial

## References
lemo-opuscar paper-lantern STYLE.md (value ladder, backlit paper, cast shadows, paper-on-a-stick motion).
Thai architecture motifs drawn generically (gable with ช่อฟ้า / ใบระกา / หางหงส์, a prang, a chedi) — no specific real temple copied.
Dancer: generic lakhon silhouette — ชฎา crown, กรรเจียก, อินทรธนู shoulder flares, bent knees, flexed fingers.

## Key light
The glowing stage screen (warm #ffc070) + the lamp flames. Backlight point (uLight) on the screen. Top key from the front for paper shadows.

## Palette / ladder (one warm temperature: dusk)
sky    #3a2418 → #9a5a32 → #e3a466 (horizon)
far    #b07a50 (trans .85)
hall   #5a3624 (trans .45)
screen #e2a35e lit, dancer #170d08
stage  #2a170e   front #0c0705
title  cream paper #efe0c0

## Typography
Trirong 600 (title) / Trirong 400 (subtitle). Elegant, no neon, no glow on text. Cream paper strips on the front band, grow from the left (clip reveal).

## Motion personality
Slow, held, deliberate (ram). Holds at each pose; upper arm leads, forearm follows ~0.12 s later, head ~0.2 s later (overlap). Lamps light left→right staggered, then the screen. Camera: slow push-in.

## Never in this shot
Neon/glow titles, blue night, ease-in-out sine loops, centered dancer (dancer on the right third), text over a lit point.
