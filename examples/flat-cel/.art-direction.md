# Art direction — slopless-2d demo (Rainhead-style test scene)

## Audience & platform
English self-improvement audience, YouTube 16:9 (1920x1080), 9:16 clips cut later.

## Scene
Late night. Someone asleep on their desk wakes, lifts their head, keeps going. Caption: "Day 41. Still here."

## Mood (3 words)
tired, quiet, stubborn

## References (what to take)
Muted indie animated shorts: flat shapes, hard-edged light pools, big wall shadows. NOT glossy 3D-ish shading.

## Key light
Desk lamp, left of the character at head height. Warm, hard. The ONLY light that makes highlights.
Window/street = flat ambient, no highlights. The laundry sign emits its own light but lights nothing in the room.

## Shadow model
Flat cel. Shadow = surface color, darker, hue shifted cooler. Big cast shadow of the character on the back wall, away from the lamp. Contact shadows on the desk.

## Palette
ink    #1F1C22  (warm near-black, lines and darkest)
paper  #E9DCC2  (lightest)
wall   #4A4F45  (olive-grey, night)
wood   #8A6A4F
cloth  #6E7B86  (sweater, desaturated slate)
accent #F2B544  (lamp light — focal only)

## Line & texture
No outlines on big shapes; ink outline 3px only on the character. Grain 0.07. Seeded variation on rain.

## Typography
Caption: Fraunces Italic, 54px, paper color, bottom-left in the 16:9 safe area, no shadow.
In-world Thai sign: Bai Jamjuree Bold, skewed to the building plane, slightly blurred (far), one flickering letter.

## Motion personality
Heavy then decisive. Slumped hold → small anticipation dip → lift with ease.enter → hold. Caption words stagger 90ms.

## Never in this project
Purple/blue night, neon glow halos, blurred black drop shadows, centered subject, Inter.
