# Art direction — "Golden Meadow" (slopless-2d, material depth: clean)

## Platform
YouTube 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, ~6 s nature establishing shot. No characters.

## Story beat (state change)
Wind arrives: the meadow is still, then a gust rolls across the grass from left to right and through the tree; light shafts brighten as clouds slide off the sun.

## Mood (3 words)
warm, calm, alive

## Key light
Low sun, back-left (behind the far treeline, slightly above it). Backlight: things facing camera are in soft shade, edges facing the sun glow. Shadows fall TOWARD the camera, down-right.

## Shadow model
Soft painterly: form shading + occlusion, long cast shadows toward the viewer, cool green-blue shade (never grey).

## Palette
sky top   #9DB4B6   sky warm #F4D59A   sun #FFF1C9
far       #B9B49A (atmospheric)
foliage   #3E5B3A shade / #A9B84F lit / #E6E07A backlit edge
grass     #6E7F3E / #C9B865 lit
water     reflects sky
accent    sun + backlit edges only

## Texture level
clean: no noise on surfaces. Detail = individual grass blades, leaf clusters, light.

## Motion
Wind gust travels left→right (phase = time − x), blades bend with tip lag, tree sways less than grass, pollen drifts, clouds slow. Camera: very slow push-in.

## Never
Rim light on things not facing the sun, noise on grass/sky, saturated cartoon green, centered tree.
