# Art direction — "Still Here" (lemo-opuscar Paper-cut Lightbox × slopless-2d)

## Audience & platform
English self-improvement audience (Rainhead-style), YouTube 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps. 5.5 s test shot.

## Story beat
A lamp clicks on in one window of a sleeping, rainy street. Someone asleep at their desk lifts their head and keeps going. Paper caption: "Day 41. Still here."

## Mood (3 words)
tired, quiet, stubborn

## References
lemo-opuscar `paper-lantern` STYLE.md: layered backlit paper, value ladder = depth, warm light only in points, cast shadows between layers.
Native move used: "Silhouette in a window" + "The lamp turns on". NOT the demo's night train, viaduct, halts or ticket.

## Key light
Inside the lit window: the desk lamp (warm, `#ffbf6a`). It is the only saturated thing. Backlight point (`uLight`) sits on that window so the paper glows most around it. Top LED key from the front casts the soft layer shadows (style rule, never off).

## Shadow model
Engine's soft paper shadows from the top key, layer onto layer. Front layers near-black, back layers lighter (ladder never broken).

## Palette / ladder (one cool temperature: green-slate, NOT blue/purple)
sky    #0a1412 → #1c2e2a
far    #5d7770  (trans .85)
mid    #34504a  (trans .45)
facade #1a2826  (trans .15)
front  #070c0b  (trans .05)
room   #c98f52 lit wall, silhouettes #140e0b
light  #ffbf6a   accent: none besides the lamp

## Typography
Paper caption: Fraunces Italic, cream paper `#e6d6b8`, cut strip on the front band, grows from the left edge (clip reveal, glyphs never squashed). No burned-in subtitle duplicate.
Thai sign: Bai Jamjuree Bold, dark letters on cream board, on the mid building across the street; its own small lamp sits ABOVE the board, never over the letters.

## Motion personality
Paper on a stick: rotation about joints only. Slumped hold → anticipation dip → decisive lift (ease.enter) → hold. Lamp cue: window glow first, then the whole stack's backlight rises (~0.5 s). Camera: slow dolly in toward the window + 0.6 mm hand drift.

## Never in this shot
Blue/purple night, neon, centered subject (window sits on the right third), scale-0 pop-ins, text over a lit point.
